# Miraculous: Tales of Ladybug & Cat Noir

## Overview
Set in modern-day **Paris**, the series follows teenagers **Marinette Dupain-Cheng** and **Adrien Agrest** as they transform into the superheroes *Ladybug* and *Cat Noir* to protect the city from villains created by Hawk Moth.

---

## Key Characters
* **Marinette Dupain-Cheng** / *Ladybug*
* **Adrien Agrest** / *Cat Noir*
* **Tikky** & **Plagg** (Kwamies)
